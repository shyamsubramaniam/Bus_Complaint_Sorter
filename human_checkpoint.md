# Human Checkpoint

## Purpose
The Bus Complaint Sorter identifies and lists complaints that may involve safety risks.

## Human Review Process
1. The program classifies the complaints.
2. Urgent safety complaints are saved in urgent.csv.
3. A transport officer reviews each urgent complaint.
4. The officer checks the original complaint and decides what action is necessary.
5. The officer records or communicates the decision.

## Important Note
The system does not automatically punish drivers, change bus routes, or take action against transport staff.

A human transport officer is responsible for reviewing complaints and making final decisions.
